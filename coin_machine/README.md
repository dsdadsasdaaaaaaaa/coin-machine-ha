# Coin Machine add-on

AI deal analyst for coin flippers, collectors and dealers, as a Home Assistant add-on.

- Searches Kijiji near you (and Facebook Marketplace once you sign in to it), reads every new ad whole, and keeps
  looking with no browser open. eBay and Reddit too, when you want them.
- Tells your phone about a new Buy, or a listing under the value of its metal, through the iPhone app or Home
  Assistant notifications.
- Keeps the figures of an account in Canada in Canadian dollars, and knows Canadian coins.
- Melt, fees, profit and verdicts are computed in code; the AI only describes what it sees.
- Password protected, with accounts for the people you give one to, at one address of your own.
- Your data stays in the add-on's folder and is included in Home Assistant backups.

It never bids, buys or messages anyone.

See the **Documentation** tab for installation and setup.
